Getting started

A quick guide to your first casts and upgrades.

1. Cast your line
Press CAST LINE to try for a fish. Each cast costs 3 Energy; Energy restores by 1 every 30 seconds. A caught fish earns EXP and enters your aquarium.
2. Grow and sell fish
Each successful catch helps the fish you are holding grow. Sell individual fish, fully grown fish, your whole aquarium, or a chosen rarity to earn Silver.
3. Improve your gear
Spend Silver on rod upgrades for more power, value, and critical hits. Aquarium upgrades add capacity and make held fish grow faster.
4. Complete quests and rebirth
Quests reward Silver or Gold and EXP. Rebirth costs Gold and resets current fishing progress, but your collection and claimed achievements remain.
